## What does a room addition contractor in {city} do?

A room addition contractor designs and builds new living space onto an existing home, such as a bedroom, primary suite, family room, or larger kitchen. In {city}, that includes coordinating plans and permits, building the foundation and structure, tying into the existing roof and utilities, and matching finishes so the addition looks original.

## What kinds of additions do we build?

### Bedroom and primary suite additions

Adding a bedroom, or a primary suite with its own bathroom and closet, is one of the most common ways families make more room without moving.

### Family rooms and great rooms

A larger gathering space off the kitchen or backyard can change how a home lives day to day. We plan these additions around natural light, flow, and how the space connects to the rest of the house.

### Bump-outs

Sometimes a few extra feet is enough. A bump-out expands a kitchen, bathroom, or dining room without the cost of a full addition.

### Second-story additions

When there's no room to build out, building up can nearly double your living space. Second-story additions require careful structural evaluation of the existing house and more involved planning.

## How do you make an addition blend in?

An addition that looks tacked on hurts both curb appeal and value. We focus on:

- **Rooflines** that follow the existing pitch and style
- **Exterior finishes** matched to your stucco, siding, and trim
- **Windows and doors** that match the style of the existing home
- **Interior details** such as flooring, ceiling height, and trim profiles
- **Comfort**, extending or adding HVAC so the new room feels like the rest of the house

## What affects the cost of a room addition in {city}?

Major factors include the size of the addition, whether it includes a bathroom or kitchen, foundation and site conditions, roof complexity, how much existing structure has to change, and your finish selections. Second-story additions also involve structural upgrades to the existing house. Our written estimate breaks costs out so you can adjust the scope to fit your budget.

## What's the process from idea to finished room?

1. **Consultation** to understand your goals, budget, and lot
2. **Design and engineering**, including energy compliance calculations
3. **Permitting**, including responding to any plan-check corrections
4. **Construction**: foundation, framing, roofing, rough utilities, and inspections
5. **Tie-in and finishes**: opening into the existing home and completing the interior
6. **Final inspection and walkthrough**

## Why choose a licensed general contractor for additions?

Additions are structural work that must meet the building code and pass multiple inspections. A licensed general contractor coordinates every trade, carries the required insurance and bonding, and is accountable to the Contractors State License Board. Always verify a contractor's license before signing a contract.

## What should I ask before hiring an addition contractor?

An addition is a major investment, so it's worth asking a few direct questions of any contractor you're considering:

- **Is your license active, and what classification is it?** You can verify this on the CSLB website.
- **Do you carry general liability and workers' compensation insurance?** Ask for certificates.
- **Who manages the project day to day?** You should know who to call with questions.
- **How do you handle change orders?** Changes should be written and priced before work proceeds.
- **What does the payment schedule look like?** Payments should track completed work, and California limits the size of down payments on home improvement contracts.
- **Who handles plans, engineering, and permits?** Make sure responsibilities are spelled out in the contract.

Clear answers up front make for a smoother project and fewer surprises along the way.
