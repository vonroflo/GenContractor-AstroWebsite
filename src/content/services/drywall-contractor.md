## What does a drywall contractor in {city} do?

A drywall contractor installs, finishes, and repairs the gypsum board that forms most interior walls and ceilings. In {city}, that ranges from patching a single hole to hanging and finishing every wall in a remodel or addition, then matching the texture so the work blends in and is ready for paint.

## What drywall services do we provide?

### New installation

For remodels, additions, ADUs, and garage conversions, we hang drywall over new framing, choosing the right board for each space: standard board for most rooms, moisture-resistant board in bathrooms and laundry rooms, and fire-rated board where code requires it, such as walls shared between a garage and living space.

### Taping and finishing

Finishing is where drywall work succeeds or fails. Seams are taped and coated in several passes, with sanding between coats, until joints disappear. The level of finish is matched to how the wall will be seen: smoother finishes for walls under strong light or with glossy paint, standard finishes behind texture.

### Repairs

We fix holes from doorknobs and moving day, cracks from settling, nail pops, and damage from plumbing leaks. For water damage, we cut out wet or stained material, confirm the leak has been fixed and the cavity has dried, and replace the section.

### Texture matching

Most homes in the area have a texture such as orange peel or knockdown. A patch that's smooth on a textured wall stands out. We match the pattern by hand and test before texturing the full repair.

## How do you keep drywall work clean?

Drywall dust gets everywhere if it isn't controlled. We:

- Cover floors and furniture before starting
- Seal off the work area where practical
- Use dust-reducing sanding methods
- Vacuum and wipe down at the end of the job

## How much does drywall work cost in {city}?

Small repairs are priced by the visit, since most of the time goes into drying between coats. Larger jobs are priced by the area of board, the level of finish, ceiling height, and any texture work. Water-damage repairs depend on how much material has to come out. We'll give you a written estimate after seeing the space.

## When is a drywall problem a sign of something bigger?

Most drywall damage is cosmetic, but some signs point to a hidden issue:

1. **Stains or soft spots** usually mean a plumbing or roof leak.
2. **Long diagonal cracks** from door or window corners can come from settling or foundation movement.
3. **Bubbling or peeling paint** on exterior walls can signal moisture getting in from outside.

In those cases, we find and fix the cause first so the repair doesn't fail.

## Why hire a contractor instead of a handyman?

For a small patch, the difference may not matter. For remodels, fire-rated assemblies, or water-damage repairs that involve finding a leak, a licensed contractor carries the license, insurance, and code knowledge to do the work correctly and pass inspection when a permit is involved.

## What are the levels of drywall finish?

The drywall industry describes finish quality in levels from 0 to 5. Knowing the terms helps you compare estimates:

- **Level 0–1**: board hung, little or no finishing. Used in attics and hidden areas.
- **Level 2**: tape embedded with one coat. Common in garages and behind tile.
- **Level 3**: two coats on joints. Suited to walls that will receive heavy texture.
- **Level 4**: three coats on joints, sanded smooth. Standard for most homes with flat paint or light texture.
- **Level 5**: a skim coat over the entire surface. Recommended under strong side lighting or glossy paint.

We'll recommend the right level for each room so you don't pay for more finish than you need, or end up with seams showing through the paint.
