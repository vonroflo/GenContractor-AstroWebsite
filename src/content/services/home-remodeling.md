## What does a home remodeling contractor in {city} do?

A home remodeling contractor plans and manages renovations to existing living space: kitchens, bathrooms, layouts, and finishes. In {city}, that means coordinating plans, pulling permits, scheduling every trade, passing inspections, and delivering a finished space on an agreed scope and budget, with one point of contact throughout.

## What remodeling projects do we take on?

### Kitchen remodeling

The kitchen is the most-used room in most homes and the most complex to remodel. Projects range from new cabinets and counters in the same layout to full redesigns that move plumbing, add an island, or open the kitchen to the living area. We coordinate cabinets, counters, appliances, lighting, and flooring so everything arrives and fits.

### Bathroom remodeling

Bathroom remodels range from updating fixtures and tile to converting a tub to a walk-in shower or reworking the layout entirely. Because bathrooms are wet spaces, we focus on what's behind the tile: proper waterproofing, ventilation, and plumbing.

### Whole-home renovations

For bigger projects, we can renovate multiple rooms or an entire home in one coordinated project, which is usually faster and more efficient than a series of separate jobs.

### Layout changes

Removing or moving walls can transform how a home feels. We find out whether a wall is load-bearing, coordinate engineering when needed, and handle any plumbing, electrical, or HVAC inside the wall.

## What should I budget for a remodel in {city}?

The biggest cost drivers are:

- **Scope**: cosmetic updates versus layout changes
- **Selections**: cabinets, counters, tile, and fixtures vary widely in price
- **Behind-the-wall work**: moving plumbing, upgrading electrical, or fixing hidden damage
- **Permits and engineering** when required

We recommend setting aside a contingency for conditions that can't be seen until walls are opened, particularly in older homes. Our estimates are itemized so you can adjust selections to fit your budget.

## How do we keep a remodel on track?

Remodels go off track when selections are late, trades aren't coordinated, or communication breaks down. We:

1. Finalize as many selections as possible before demolition
2. Build a schedule that accounts for material lead times and inspections
3. Keep one point of contact for questions and decisions
4. Put every change in writing before work proceeds

## What about living at home during construction?

Most clients stay home during a remodel. We protect floors and furniture, contain dust, and keep the site tidy at the end of each day. For kitchen projects, we'll help you plan where to set up a temporary kitchen.

## Why does hiring a licensed contractor matter for remodeling?

Remodels often touch structure, plumbing, electrical, and waterproofing, all of which are governed by building codes. A licensed general contractor can pull permits, coordinate licensed trades, and is accountable to the Contractors State License Board. You can verify any contractor's license on the CSLB website.

## How should I prepare for a remodel?

A bit of preparation makes the project smoother for everyone:

- **Collect ideas.** Photos of kitchens, bathrooms, and finishes you like help us understand your style quickly.
- **List your must-haves and nice-to-haves.** This makes it easier to adjust the scope if the budget gets tight.
- **Think about timing.** Holidays, guests, and school schedules all affect when a remodel is least disruptive.
- **Clear the work area.** Removing personal items before demolition protects them and keeps the start date on track.
- **Plan for pets and kids.** Construction areas aren't safe for either, so it helps to plan where they'll be during working hours.

We'll give you a pre-construction checklist specific to your project so you know what to expect before the first day of work.
