## How do we work?

Every project, large or small, follows the same principles:

- **Clear scope before work starts.** You get a written, itemized estimate so you know exactly what's included.
- **No surprise charges.** If something changes, we write it up and price it before we do the work.
- **One point of contact.** You always know who to call, and we return calls.
- **Respect for your home.** We protect floors and finishes, keep dust contained, and clean up every day.
- **Work done to code.** We pull permits when the work requires them and schedule every inspection.
