This Privacy Policy explains how {legalName} ("{business}," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit this website or contact us through it.

## What information do we collect?

**Information you give us.** When you request an estimate or contact us, we collect what you enter in the form: your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, the service you're interested in, project details, and your preferred contact method.

**Information collected automatically.** When you submit a form, we record the page you submitted it from and, if present in the link you followed, campaign parameters such as `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `gclid`. These tell us how you found us. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, for security and reliability.

**Spam protection.** Our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from automated bots. Turnstile may process information about your browser and device for this purpose. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.

## How do we use your information?

We use the information you provide to:

- Respond to your request and schedule estimates
- Communicate with you about your project
- Keep records of estimates and work we perform
- Understand which marketing channels bring us customers
- Protect our website against spam and abuse

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Text messages (SMS)

If you check the SMS consent box on our form, you agree to receive text messages from {business} at the phone number you provided about your inquiry, estimate, and project, such as appointment confirmations and updates. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

- Reply **STOP** to any message to opt out.
- Reply **HELP** for help, or call {phone}.

We do not share your phone number or SMS consent with third parties for their marketing purposes.

## Who do we share information with?

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, such as our website host, our customer relationship management (CRM) system, and our spam-protection provider. They may use it only to provide services to us. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

## How long do we keep your information?

We keep inquiry and project records for as long as needed to respond to you, complete your project, meet warranty and legal obligations, and keep reasonable business records.
